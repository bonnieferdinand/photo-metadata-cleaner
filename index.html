Photo Metadata Cleaner

Strip EXIF, GPS, XMP, IPTC, AI prompts and C2PA Content Credentials from JPG, PNG and WebP images. The image pixels are copied byte for byte, so quality never changes.

Runs in your browser. Images are never uploaded.

JPG · JPEG · PNG · WEBP

What gets removed

Camera make, model and serial number, GPS location, dates, author and copyright fields, editing history, Stable Diffusion and ComfyUI prompts, Midjourney and DALL·E tags, IPTC "digital source type" AI labels, and signed C2PA manifests.

What stays

The compressed image data, transparency, animation frames, and optionally the color profile and rotation flag. Nothing is re-encoded, so the cleaned file is the same picture with fewer bytes.

What metadata cleaning can't do

Some generators also hide a watermark inside the pixels themselves (for example Google SynthID). That is part of the image, not its metadata, so no lossless cleaner removes it.
